Things I've learned playing ATM11

Not expert advice, just what has made my own run smoother.

Put Ultimine on Shift + F

Sneak plus F is easy to reach mid-mining, and it keeps Ultimine from firing when you don't want it.

Give Crafting on a Stick its own key

I use numpad Enter. On a smaller keyboard, Home or a mouse side button works just as well.

Jetpack on and off with Page Up / Page Down

Separate keys mean you always know which state you're switching to, which matters over lava.
